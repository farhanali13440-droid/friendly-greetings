# Premium Pediatric & Neonatal Website Redesign

## Direction
Use the selected **Boutique Medical Suite** direction as the foundation: editorial medical typography, crisp navy surfaces, refined teal and sky-blue accents, generous whitespace, restrained borders, and modest corner radii. Adapt it away from the prototype’s sample gold styling and unsupported claims so the result feels warm, clinically credible, and distinctly branded around Dr. Ateeq Ur Rehman.

## What will be preserved
- Keep the existing single-page route and every working call, WhatsApp, directions, review, FAQ, and expandable-service interaction.
- Preserve the verified clinic address, phone numbers, email, timings, PMDC number, doctor qualifications, hospital experience, medical content, disclaimers, and current Google Maps review/directions link.
- Retain the existing doctor photograph for the physician profile, without altering its meaning or inventing new credentials.
- Keep the explicit clarification that vaccination services are not currently provided.

## Redesign scope
1. **Design system and typography**
   - Build a semantic navy, teal, sky, white, and pale blue-grey token system with accessible contrast.
   - Use an elegant editorial display face paired with a highly legible sans serif; load fonts correctly through the document head.
   - Replace bright-blue repetition, oversized rounded cards, blur effects, and excessive pills with sharper layouts, fine rules, controlled shadows, and consistent spacing.

2. **Sticky navigation and mobile menu**
   - Brand the header around **Dr. Ateeq Ur Rehman**, with Arham Childcare Clinic as the practice location.
   - Add the requested section links: Home, About, Pediatric Services, Neonatal Care, Parent Resources, Testimonials, FAQs, and Contact.
   - Add a prominent appointment action, an accessible mobile menu, smooth anchor navigation, and a more compact scrolled state.

3. **First screen**
   - Rebuild the first screen with the exact requested specialization, headline, supporting copy, doctor name, FCPS credential, trust statement, Book an Appointment, and WhatsApp actions.
   - Create a high-resolution, natural neonatal-care photograph for the dominant visual, with no distressing or invasive scene and no text covering faces.
   - Keep the image treatment refined and ensure the doctor identity, clinic location, and booking path are immediately clear.
   - Remove blur masks and translucent overlays from readability-critical areas.

4. **Doctor profile and credibility**
   - Give the existing doctor photograph a premium physician-profile presentation.
   - Preserve MBBS, FCPS (Pediatrics), PMDC Registration No. 74532-P, and the two verified ex-Registrar roles.
   - Present factual trust anchors only, including the existing 10+ years claim; no new awards, statistics, memberships, or medical claims.

5. **Pediatric and neonatal care**
   - Separate the current combined service content into a clear pediatric-services area and a visually stronger **Specialized Neonatal Care** area.
   - Use only services already stated on the website, including newborn assessment, jaundice, feeding, prematurity, low-birth-weight follow-up, infection assessment, growth, development, and warning-sign counselling.
   - Keep detailed service disclosure accessible while making the initial view concise and easy to scan.

6. **Supporting sections**
   - Refine the care approach, common concerns, clinic facilities, vaccination guidance, appointment steps, resources, reviews, FAQs, clinic information, contact area, and footer into one cohesive system.
   - Keep the existing three parent-resource previews rather than inventing medical articles; remove placeholder-style promises such as “build a library.”
   - Preserve all four existing testimonials verbatim and retain the link to Google reviews.
   - Keep clinic photo placeholders clearly neutral because no verified clinic photographs currently exist.

7. **Contact and mobile usability**
   - Keep Call, WhatsApp, Appointment, and Get Directions available at natural decision points.
   - Refine the three mobile floating actions so they remain thumb-friendly without obscuring content.
   - Ensure the full page works at 360px and across tablet, desktop, and large desktop widths with no clipping or horizontal scrolling.

8. **SEO, accessibility, and performance**
   - Add route-level title, description, Open Graph metadata, canonical URL, and local physician/medical-clinic structured data for `https://drateeqpediatrics.com` while preserving crawler access.
   - Use one H1, semantic sections, descriptive image alt text, clear focus states, keyboard-operable navigation/accordions, labelled icon actions, and reduced-motion support.
   - Optimize the neonatal image, reserve image dimensions, lazy-load below-the-fold media, and avoid unnecessary dependencies.
